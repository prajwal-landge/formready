Comprehensive Guide: Meeting Government Exam Passport Photo Criteria

In recent years, recruitment portals operated by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC), the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), and the National Testing Agency (NTA) have transitioned toward automated AI image processing. When a candidate uploads a photograph during registration, backend microservices inspect pixel resolution, header metadata, compression ratios, and facial bounding boxes before submitting the record to the database.

Key Technical Specifications Required by Indian Portals

Understanding the relationship between physical measurements, digital pixels, and compression limits is crucial for successful submission:

  • Physical Metric: Most official notifications describe passport photos as 3.5 cm in width by 4.5 cm in height.
  • Digital Resolution: On web intake systems, this standard physical aspect ratio translates to 200 x 230 pixels (SSC, IBPS, SBI) or 350 x 350 pixels (UPSC). For high-density document scanning (e.g., Passport Seva Kendra or GATE), it translates to 413 x 531 pixels at 300 DPI.
  • File Size Constraints: File limits generally range between a minimum of 20 KB and a maximum of 50 KB for SSC/IBPS, and up to 100 KB–200 KB for NTA (NEET/JEE) and UPSC.
  • Encapsulation Format: Exclusively JPEG / JPG. Portals will actively reject PNG or PDF files submitted into photograph input nodes.

The 80% Facial Bounding Box Rule

One of the most frequent rejection flags issued by the NTA and UPSC is "insufficient facial coverage". Guidelines state that the candidate's face—from the base of the chin to the top of the forehead—must occupy between 70% and 80% of the vertical frame. A common amateur mistake is submitting a full upper-body portrait where the head appears tiny and indistinguishable when scaled down to a 200-pixel thumbnail.

With FormReady’s interactive Face Zoom slider and pan controls, you can effortlessly zoom into your original smartphone snapshot until your facial features fill roughly three-quarters of the preview canvas, ensuring instant compliance with biometric matching systems.

Checklist to Avoid Common Rejection Errors

  1. Background Contrast: Ensure your backdrop is clean white or uniform off-white. Avoid walls with shadows, wallpapers, doorways, or natural scenery.
  2. Headwear & Accessories: Caps, hats, hoodies, and colored spectacles are strictly disallowed. Head coverings worn for religious reasons are permitted only if the entire facial oval (forehead, eyes, nose, cheeks, and chin) remains completely visible and unobstructed.
  3. Recency: Notifications mandate that photographs must be taken within 3 months (or 10 days in the case of UPSC CSE) prior to the notification date.
  4. Date and Name Placard (NTA Exams): For NEET UG and specific state exams, candidates must have their name and photo date legibly printed across the lower third of the photo. Check your exam cycle’s official information brochure to confirm if this rule applies.
