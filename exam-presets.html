How to Read and Apply Official Examination Document Criteria

Every examination conducting agency in India issues an official notification or Information Bulletin outlining the eligibility criteria and online application rules. While modern portals share similar technical principles, each agency establishes its own exact pixel dimensions and KB boundaries.

Verification with Official Notification Mandatory

Important Advisory for All Candidates: The specifications presented in this directory reflect official notifications from recent exam cycles. However, examination authorities occasionally update their specifications between recruitment years (for instance, changing photo size from 200x230 to 350x350, or revising max KB from 50KB to 100KB). Always cross-check your active examination cycle's official notification before submitting your application.

Why Dimensions Differ Across Portals

Candidates frequently wonder why SSC uses 200x230 px while UPSC uses 350x350 px and GATE uses 480x640 px:

  • Admit Card Layouts: SSC and IBPS format admit card templates with a portrait rectangle slot (aspect ratio ~ 1:1.15). UPSC formats their admit cards with a square photo box (aspect ratio 1:1).
  • Screen vs Print DPI: Portals like PAN Card (NSDL) utilize 300 Dots Per Inch (DPI) print-density standards to manufacture physical plastic cards with laser-engraved photographs, necessitating strict 213x213 pixel dimensions.
  • OCR Biometric Scanning: NTA uses computerized face detection on entrance exam admit cards to detect proxy test-takers, demanding higher resolution (10KB–200KB) and 80% face coverage.
