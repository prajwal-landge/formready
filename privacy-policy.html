Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026. At FormReady, we consider the privacy of our visitors to be of paramount importance. This document details our zero-upload architecture and our third-party advertising disclosures.

1. Absolute Zero-Upload Document Privacy Guarantee

Unlike conventional cloud-based conversion platforms that transmit user files across the internet to remote application servers, FormReady operates under a strict client-side execution model.

When you select, drag, drop, paste, crop, resize, or compress a photograph, digital signature, or identification card using our website:

  • No File Transmission: Your image files are read directly into your device's browser runtime memory using the HTML5 FileReader and CanvasRenderingContext2D APIs. Zero bytes of your image data are transmitted to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.
  • No Server Storage or Logging: FormReady maintains no backend database, cloud storage buckets (such as Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage), or temporary caching directories for user media. We do not have access to your images, nor can we view, replicate, or recover them.
  • Immediate Memory Clearance: When you refresh the tab, close the browser window, or click "Change Photo", the local blob object URL is immediately revoked (via URL.revokeObjectURL()) and purged by your browser's garbage collection.

2. Standard Web Server Log Files

FormReady follows standard operational procedures of utilizing hosting log files. These files log visitors when they visit web pages—a routine function implemented by all web hosting providers (such as Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, or GitHub Pages) for infrastructure analytics and operational security.

Information collected inside standard server logs includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser brand and version (User-Agent string)
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps of page requests
  • Referring and exit page URLs
  • Number of clicks and navigational paths across the site

None of this server logging information is linked to any personally identifiable information (PII). The primary purpose of this telemetry is analyzing aggregate traffic trends, administering the site, diagnosing network outages, and ensuring optimal load delivery.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most websites, FormReady uses 'cookies'. Cookies are tiny text files stored on your computer or mobile device by your web browser. We use cookies and local storage tokens to remember your user preferences (such as your chosen Dark or Light color mode preference via localStorage) and to evaluate which subpages of the site visitors access most frequently.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Vendors Disclosures

To keep FormReady 100% free for students and job applicants without imposing paywalls or subscription charges, we partner with third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense.

As a condition of our participation in the Google AdSense program, we provide the following required disclosures regarding advertising cookies:

  • Third-Party Vendors: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on FormReady (https://formready.io).
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google’s use of the DoubleClick DART cookie enables it and its advertising partners to serve targeted, interest-based advertisements to our visitors based on their visit to FormReady and other sites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on FormReady, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by these third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.
  • No Control Over Third-Party Cookies: FormReady has no access to or control over cookies that are used by third-party advertisers. We strongly advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad vendors for more detailed information.

How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising and DART Cookies

You have the complete right and ability to disable personalized advertising cookies at any time:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): You can opt out of a wide array of third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative Consumer Opt-Out Page.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): You may also exercise your opt-out preferences across participating advertising networks by visiting the AboutAds Consumer Choice Tool.
  • Browser Cookie Controls: You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that disabling cookies may affect your browser’s ability to persist preferences like your dark mode selection.

5. CCPA and GDPR Privacy Rights

Under applicable data protection frameworks—including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)—users possess specific fundamental rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to request disclosure of what personal data an enterprise holds concerning you. Because FormReady does not collect, record, or retain your personal photos, signatures, or biographical data, we hold zero personal records about your identity.
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of any personal data. Because our tools process data locally in client memory and write nothing to remote disks, all uploaded image data is wiped from your local memory the moment you leave or close the page.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: You will never be discriminated against for exercising any privacy or cookie opt-out right on FormReady.

6. Children's Information (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children online is especially important to us. FormReady does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that FormReady inadvertently possesses personally identifiable information of a child in its server logs, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records from our infrastructure logs.
