Requesting a New Exam Preset

If your state public service commission (such as BPSC, TNPSC, WBPSC, OPSC, or GPSC), local university, or regional recruitment board has unique photo and signature upload requirements not yet listed in our directory, please include:

  • Official Examination Name and Conducting Authority
  • Exact Pixel Dimensions (Width × Height)
  • File Size Constraints (Minimum and Maximum KB)
  • Accepted Formats (e.g. JPG, JPEG, PNG)
  • Link to the official employment advertisement or Information Bulletin

Our team verifies the details against the official gazette and adds the new preset to our interactive selector within 48 hours.

Reporting Technical Issues with Image Resizing

Because FormReady processes images client-side within your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API, technical glitches are typically related to device memory limitations or browser version incompatibilities. When reporting an issue, please mention:

  • The device you are using (e.g. Android smartphone, iPhone, Windows PC, or Mac)
  • Your web browser and version (e.g. Google Chrome 124, Safari 17, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge)
  • The approximate dimensions and file size of your original source photograph
  • The specific error message or toast notification shown by the tool
